Rooftop photovoltaic systems for industrial platforms, logistics halls, and
production facilities — sized to your real consumption profile and roof
structure, not a generic package.

## How it works

We start with a site assessment: roof structure, orientation, shading, and
your actual monthly consumption. From there we design a system sized for
self-consumption first, so the energy your roof produces offsets what your
facility already uses.

## What's included

- Site assessment and structural review
- System design and sizing
- Installation and commissioning by our own crews, to ANRE grid-connection
  requirements
- Ongoing maintenance and monitoring after handover

Rooftop solar is one of three systems we deliver end to end — see also
