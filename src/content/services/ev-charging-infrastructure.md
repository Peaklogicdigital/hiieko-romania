## How it works

Integrated into new or existing solar and battery installations, sized to
your fleet or facility's actual charging needs, not sold as a separate,
disconnected system. Retail charging stations can also be integrated where
the objective is to generate additional revenue by offering charging
services to customers.

- Site assessment
- Installation
- Integration with your existing (or new) solar/BESS system
- Ongoing maintenance under the same single contract as the rest of your
  system
