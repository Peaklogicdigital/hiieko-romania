Industrial battery energy storage systems (BESS) for peak shaving, backup
power during outages, and capturing the value of stored energy alongside a
solar installation.

## How it works

We size a battery system to your facility's load profile and, where
relevant, alongside a rooftop or ground-mount solar installation, designed,
installed, and maintained under the same one-contract model as our solar
work.

## What's included

- Site assessment and load profiling
- System design and sizing
- Installation and commissioning
- Ongoing maintenance and monitoring after handover
