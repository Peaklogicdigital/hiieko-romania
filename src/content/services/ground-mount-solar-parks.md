Ground-mount solar parks on unproductive or unused land — from feasibility
study through design, installation, and grid connection.

## How it works

We assess the land, grid capacity, and permitting path, then design a
ground-mount system to match. Our own crews handle installation and
commissioning; the system stays our responsibility after handover through a
scheduled maintenance plan.

## What's included

- Feasibility study and site assessment
- System design and sizing
- Installation and commissioning to ANRE grid-connection requirements
- Ongoing maintenance and monitoring after handover
